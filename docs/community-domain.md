# FLIHub Community Domain

FLIHub Community will support participant-facing services after the League and Business foundations mature.

Potential concepts include PlayerProfile, Membership, CommunityProgram, Facility, FacilityReservation, Event, DigitalGoods, Academy, and Coach.

No Community functionality is implemented in Phase 0. The `@flihub/community` package exists only to reserve a clear architectural boundary and prevent participant-facing concerns from leaking into League or Business code.
