# Roadmap

## Phase 0: Architecture, domain modeling, and development standards

Establish TypeScript standards, documentation, package boundaries, workflow conventions, Codespaces support, and small proof-of-concept domain workflows.

## Phase 1: FLIHub League professional operations

Expand League operations around tournaments, players, teams, memberships, registrations, eligibility, rankings, schedules, scores, results, officials, staff, and prize structures.

## Phase 2: FLIHub Business internal operations and commercial capabilities

Expand departments, projects, reimbursements, budgets, vendors, approval workflows, staff assignments, reporting, sponsors, advertisers, campaigns, media rights, invoices, and payments.

## Phase 3: FLIHub Community

Introduce participant-facing programs, memberships, facilities, reservations, events, digital goods, academy features, and coaching concepts.

## Phase 4: PocketBase integration and production persistence

Add production persistence adapters while preserving domain independence from PocketBase or any other backend.

## Phase 5: Multi-tenant deployment and external licensing

Prepare FLIHub for deployment and licensing to organizations outside FGL with tenant isolation and operational controls.
